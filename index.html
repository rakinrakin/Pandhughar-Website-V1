Pandughar in numbers

United by values, driven by purpose. We are One Family, rooted in trust, respect and a shared love for hard work.

It began in 1995 with Urban Design & Development, founded by Mr. Nazim Uddin Ahmed and named after his ancestral village in Cumilla. Today the group spans garments, consumer goods, real estate, building services, finance, technology and agro, opening doors to Many Futures: sustainable, responsible and impactful.

Growing together,giving back.

Where our name comes from

We give back where our name began.

Most of our charity work happens in Pandughar village, Muradnagar, and the char areas of northern Bangladesh, with quarterly reports on every programme.

Where the support goes

Four programmes, run year-round by the Pandughar family.

Education

0+

Students in free education

Pandughar Samsuddin Ahammad Bhuiyan High School gives free schooling for classes 6 to 10, alongside primary schooling, scholarships and basic education for garment workers.

Food security

0+

Families supported every month

Monthly rice and cash support reaches families in Pandughar village, Gaibandha, Char Rajibpur in Kurigram and Chitalmari in Bagerhat.

Safe water

Char

Tube wells for river islands

New tube wells bring safe drinking water to the char areas of Char Rajibpur, Kurigram, where clean water is hardest to reach.

Relief & care

24/7

Disaster relief & medical support

Aid after natural calamities, winter clothing drives, and medical support for staff and their families, including cancer, cardiac and surgical care.
